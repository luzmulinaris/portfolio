Most of my favourite projects started on paper. Moving them to the screen taught
me which habits to keep and which to drop.

**Keep:** a strong grid, real hierarchy, and the discipline of preparing files
properly. A magazine spread and a landing page both reward restraint.

**Drop:** the idea that a layout is ever "final". On screen the canvas changes
size constantly, so the system has to bend without breaking.

The bridge between the two is type. If the typography is right, the work feels
like mine whether it's printed at A4 or rendered on a phone.
